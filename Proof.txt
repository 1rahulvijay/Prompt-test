Build a high-fidelity, interactive HTML prototype for an enterprise agentic analytics platform called ProofLine AI.

Product concept

ProofLine AI is an autonomous analytics investigation and evidence platform designed for regulated financial institutions.

When a KPI, report or dataset changes unexpectedly, the platform:

* Investigates the movement across source systems and transformations.
* Distinguishes genuine business movement from data or technology issues.
* Generates and tests competing root-cause hypotheses.
* Identifies affected portfolios, reports, controls and stakeholders.
* Produces an evidence-backed conclusion.
* Creates technical, management and governance summaries.
* Requires human approval before remediation or external communication.

This is not a generic chatbot or text-to-SQL interface. It should feel like a serious analytics investigation control centre.

Prototype objective

Demonstrate how an analyst can:

1. See an unexpected KPI movement.
2. Launch an agentic investigation.
3. Watch specialised agents analyse the issue.
4. Review the proposed root cause.
5. inspect supporting evidence and lineage.
6. Understand downstream impact.
7. Approve, challenge or escalate the conclusion.
8. Generate an executive-ready incident summary.

Use completely synthetic banking data. Do not use real company names, logos, customer information or confidential data.

Technical requirements

Create the prototype as a single, self-contained index.html file.

* Embed all HTML, CSS and JavaScript in the file.
* Do not require a backend or build process.
* Use vanilla JavaScript.
* Use inline SVG for icons and visualisations where practical.
* Avoid external dependencies unless absolutely necessary.
* All buttons and interactions must work.
* Use realistic mock data.
* Make the application responsive, but optimise it for a 1440px enterprise desktop screen.
* Persist simple UI state during the session.
* Add subtle transitions, loading states, tooltips and hover effects.
* Do not create a static screenshot disguised as an application.

Visual direction

Use a sophisticated enterprise analytics design:

* Dark navy application shell.
* Slightly lighter navy content surfaces.
* White and light-grey primary text.
* Cyan or teal for validated information.
* Amber for warnings.
* Red for critical findings.
* Green for completed validations.
* Subtle borders, shadows and depth.
* Limited glassmorphism only for overlays and important cards.
* Compact but readable typography similar to Inter.
* Dense information presentation suitable for professional analysts.
* Avoid excessive gradients, neon effects, oversized cards and generic AI imagery.
* The product should feel trustworthy, controlled and audit-ready.

Important product principles

Communicate these principles throughout the interface:

* Evidence before explanation.
* Deterministic validation before AI conclusions.
* Read-only investigation by default.
* Least-privilege access.
* No production remediation without approval.
* No unsupported claims.
* Complete auditability.
* Clear separation between confirmed facts, hypotheses and unresolved questions.

Final output
